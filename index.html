Visuomotor adaptation

Tail movements control visual feedback. This example shows the transition from normal to reduced feedback gain in the same larva.

The green line follows the tail. Moving gratings provide visual feedback in the head-restrained, closed-loop paradigm. This video illustrates the setup and trial transition; group comparisons are presented in the poster.
